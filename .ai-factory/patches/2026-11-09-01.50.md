# Sequential numbering for competitor cards

**Date:** 2026-11-09
**Files:** dist/real-preparation.js, tests/browser-workflow-v3.js
**Severity:** low

## Problem

Competitor cards displayed 01 00 02 03 06 04 05 07 instead of consecutive numbers.

## Root Cause

The renderer used the board stage ordinal helper on a competitor array index. Stable stage IDs have a deliberately reordered mapping and omit legacy stage 1, so that helper cannot number unrelated lists.

## Solution

Display the one-based competitor list position. Extend the existing v3 browser fixture to eight competitors and assert their visible sequence. The browser check reproduced the incorrect sequence before the implementation change.

## Prevention

Keep stage-ID-to-display-position mapping limited to stage navigation. Check list numbering with multiple entries, including entries beyond the stage count; a one-card fixture hides this error.
